The Orbitals Live Wallpaper.<br />
<br />
<br />
Orbitals LWP is the second spin-off from my original Target Live Wallpaper app.<br />
<br />
Orbitals introduces a windows 8 style loading bubbles along with standard and knotted orbits (inspired by the Trefoil knot).<br />
<br />
The color schemes are mostly tech/oss inspired and include Ubuntu, XDA, Slashdot and more.<br />
<br />
The color scheme, trail length and speed of the orbitals will vary randomly and changed after every touch.<br />
<br />
Let me know what you thought out even better let every one know by rating in the Google Play Store or donating.<br />
<br />
So get cracking and check out <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=83A9zcTSBV0">the video</a> below, the <a href="https://github.com/PuZZleDucK/Orbital-Live-Wallpaper/">source code</a> and <a href="http://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=orbitlivewallpaper.puzzleduck.com">the app itself</a> in the market (or have a look at <a href="http://play.google.com/store/search?q=pub:PuZZleDucK+Industries.&amp;c=apps">my other work</a>)<br />
