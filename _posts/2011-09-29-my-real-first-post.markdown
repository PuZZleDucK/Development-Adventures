<div><p>So I'm starting a blog about Android development, last night I had a particularly intriguing battle with app inventor and the Android market, but I'll get to that later :D</p>
<p>Here's a brief summary of my life so far in bullet point form:</p>
<p>-started with a g1, or as us Aussies call it: the dream...and I still dream about that awesome flip out keyboard.<br>
-did all the tutorials and stuff, did the nehe open gl tutorials, and... Never really got anything to a publishable state.<br>
-did a few app inventor projects when it first came out, they languished in obscurity until now.<br>
-upgraded to the desire.<br>
-started porting 3D chess to Android.<br>
-started releasing live wallpapers to Android market.<br>
-got the 3D chess project running in a proof-of-code sort of way and haven't worked much on it recently.<br>
-upgraded to Telstra t-touch (or to the rest of the world: the ideos w8)... Damn you for not releasing honeycomb google.<br>
-experimentally added add mob adds to one of my apps.<br>
-topped over 1000 users for my live wallpapers with majority good reviews but very few comments.<br>
-add mob revenue still a good 10c short of a dollar... Plenty of impressions, so they are bring seen but not used... Fair enough.<br>
-started exporting app inventor apps to the Android marketplace... Hell of a night :P<br>
-started blogging about it.</p>
